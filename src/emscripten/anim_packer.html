Animation packer

Turns a set of frame PNGs into the two files the engine loads: a vertically packed sprite sheet and its .anim frame table (see TOOLS.md). Commit both under the adventure's assets/common/<dir>/ — sprite sheets are art, not text, so they do not belong in a locale layer. Everything happens in the browser; nothing is uploaded.
A talking sheet driven by lip-sync cues needs exactly 7 frames, in the order X A B C D E F (see SPEECH.md); the packer cannot check that for you.

Drop frame PNGs here, or click to choose files.
